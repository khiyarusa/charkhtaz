## Identity checklist

1. **Hofmeister kink** — the forward-hooked bend at the base of the rear side window's trailing edge. The single strongest G20 cue. **[confirmed — BMW design signature, visible on every G20]**
2. **Long bonnet and cab-rearward stance**, with the windscreen base well behind the front axle (u +0.245 versus axle +0.315). **[confirmed — S5 proportions; value estimated]**
3. **Sharp shoulder line** running from the top of the front wing, through both door handles, into the tail lamps. **[confirmed — G20 body-side feature]**
4. **Lower door crease** above the sill, fading into the rear door. **[interpretation]**
5. **Slim, horizontal L-shaped tail lamps** wrapping a short distance onto the rear quarter. **[confirmed — pre-LCI lamp shape]**
6. **High-gloss Shadowline window surround** (Sport Line) with a black B-pillar and body-colour roof mouldings. **[confirmed — S1 Sport Line equipment]**
7. **"Air Breather" vent** behind the front wheel arch, a vertical slash in the wing. **[confirmed — G20 feature]**
8. **18-inch V-spoke style 780 bicolour wheels**, Orbit Grey with a gloss-lathed face. **[confirmed — S1]**
9. **Shark-fin antenna** on the rear roof. **[confirmed — repo `detail.antenna`, and visible on G20]**
10. **Flush pull-type door handles** sitting on the shoulder line rather than above it. **[interpretation]**

The roster's tyre string (225/45 R18) matches the 18-inch Sport Line fitment, so the art should use style 780 rather than the 17-inch 776. On UK cars the 18s were standard on Sport; in Germany they were an option on top of the standard 17s (S2).

## Facelift and market notes

- **Pre-LCI: October 2018 – mid-2022** (2019–2022 model years). Sport Line exists in this period only. The 2021 car is pre-LCI: pre-LCI headlamp internals, front bumper and tail-lamp graphic.
- **LCI: from July 2022** (2023 MY). New headlamps with inverted-L DRLs, redesigned front and rear bumpers, curved display inside. The **side view is unchanged** — same doors, shoulder line, Hofmeister kink and roof. Only wheel designs and trim finishes differ.
- **Do not mix LCI dimensions:** the LCI 320i is 4,713 mm long and 1,440 mm tall (S3, S4), i.e. +4 mm and +5 mm against the 2021 car. Small, but the shape is normalised to length, so it shifts every u value slightly.
- **Market:** Sport Line was sold across Europe and most export markets; the US did not get a 320i Sport Line in 2021 (US 330i/330e only). The roster's "international" label should mean European spec, which is also what Iranian importers sourced.
- 2021 320i output is 184 hp in Europe. Some markets (Turkey, Tunisia) sold a 1.6-litre 170 hp 320i — not this car (S5).
